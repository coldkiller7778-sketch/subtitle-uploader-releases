# Subtitle Studio Pro

**Subtitle Studio Pro** is an internal desktop application used by authorized staff for YouTube channel operations, multilingual subtitle management, and localized video metadata management.

This application uses **YouTube API Services** and **Google OAuth 2.0** to manage YouTube channels that our team is authorized to operate.

We provide YouTube channel operation and localization services for authorized content partners, including **DramaBox**.

## Privacy & Terms

- [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md)
- [Terms of Use](TERMS.md)

## Main Functions

Subtitle Studio Pro helps authorized channel operators manage multilingual YouTube publishing workflows, including:

- Uploading and updating multilingual subtitles
- Checking existing caption and subtitle tracks
- Managing localized video titles and descriptions
- Retrieving basic video and channel information
- Managing authorized YouTube channels through Google OAuth 2.0
- Supporting multilingual metadata publishing
- Reducing repetitive manual operations in YouTube Studio
- Improving the accuracy and efficiency of multilingual content management

## Authorized YouTube Channel Operations

Subtitle Studio Pro is used only for YouTube channels, videos, and content that our staff are authorized to access and manage.

Users authenticate their Google/YouTube accounts through Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization process.

The application may access information required for authorized channel-management functions, including:

- YouTube channel information
- Video IDs and basic video metadata
- Existing caption tracks
- Multilingual subtitles
- Localized video titles and descriptions
- OAuth authorization information required to perform authorized operations

The application does not request or store users' Google or YouTube passwords.

## Content Partners

Our team provides YouTube channel operation and localization services for authorized content partners, including **DramaBox**.

Our responsibilities may include:

- Preparing and uploading multilingual subtitles
- Localizing titles and descriptions
- Managing authorized video metadata
- Supporting international content distribution
- Maintaining multilingual publishing workflows

All YouTube content managed through Subtitle Studio Pro must be authorized by the relevant channel owner or content rights holder.
